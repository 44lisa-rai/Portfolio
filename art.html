Macho Paiting

The macho and mini painting next to it are a set. Our goal was to construct canvas by cutting wood, stapling it, stretching canvas, and then gessoing it. Then our prompt was to connect the two paintings, and I chose to depict a scene from my book. This is Kendall and Hollis before the main conflict, shown with serene cool-toned colors.

Mini Painting

The mini painting is the continutation of the macho painting, and displays Kendall after a major conflict with Holly. She is at her desk, tired from the drama but still surrounded by elements of Hollis. The painting is very warm and bright to contrast the calm of the cool toned painting before it.

Self Protrait

The prompt of this piece was to display your true self. I took it a step further into an analysis of how I often take a lot of work and put in effort to get it all done, but sometimes it can leave me overwhelmed and exhausted. It focuses on the side of me no peers can see because it only occurs with myself in the late night, which is the reason I thought it would be perfect to display as a "self portrait".
