Projects and Research Highlights

ARIA Labs - Colorado School of MINES

This was an internship opportunity done at the Colorado School of MINES where I worked in a software group to research autonomous robots. We accomplished studying and writing documentation on ROS2, Colcon, and LiDAR sensors.

AP Computer Science P - Final Project

In my junior year of high school, I took AP Computer Science Principles. As a part of the class, I had to submit a project that demonstrated my understanding of basic Python programming. I tailored the project to resemble a study timer, which is a tool I use daily to keep myself productive.
